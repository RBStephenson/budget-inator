Budget-inator field guide

First-time setup

Go to Settings and fill in your net salary, pay frequency, first paycheck date, and current balance.

Then add your bills from the dashboard or the Bills page.

Tracking payments

  • ✓ Paid — marks the bill paid for this period.
  • — Skip — excludes the amount from the running balance.
  • ↺ Undo — resets back to pending.

Entering an actual amount updates in place — no more jumping back to the top of the page.

Colour isn't the only signal

Every bill status shows an icon and a text label alongside its colour, so status is readable in grayscale or by screen reader.
